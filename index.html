Backup and restore

Records are stored in this browser only. Clearing the browser's data would erase them, so download a backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe, such as a flash drive. A backup contains student and payment records, so keep it private.

Export for Excel

Spreadsheet copies for viewing or printing. They cannot be restored; use the full backup for that.

Restore from a backup
